| R-032 | The S2-02 approval state machine is a proof of concept: nothing stores or calls it. Concurrent decisions are not proven: the class is not thread-safe, and version checks were only tested one call at a time. Actors and delegations are trusted from a calling layer that does not exist yet. One person passing the domain checks can choose the approvers of every held stage on a request, including mandatory and independent ones, and authenticating the actor alone will not close this (DL-048). There are no correlation ids. A wrong time inside the accepted range can freeze an instance. Evidence is not retained or tamper-evident, and approver resolution, workgroups, reevaluation and timeout scheduling are not built, so a timeout does nothing until something applies it | M | H | Keep it labelled as a proof of concept; before any use settle DL-048, and add storage with optimistic concurrency and a race test on both engines, the audit writer, authenticated actors and verified delegations, approver resolution, a timeout scheduler, and the workflow/domain and Security Architect approvals | ADR-0005, DL-010, DL-013, DL-042, DL-043, DL-044, DL-045, DL-046, DL-047, DL-048 | IAM Domain lead, Security Architect | Open |
| R-031 | The S2-01 policy evaluator is a proof of concept: nothing stores, publishes or calls it. Limits are provisional (DL-008) and timing is one local measurement. Evidence lacks a snapshot reference, synchronisation times and identities, so a freshness decision cannot yet be reconstructed; worst case is 6.7 MB; a condition's result plus the rule's literal can reveal a sensitive value. Overlap detection is conservative, partly covers IAM-WF-016, and is untested on real matrices. Policy keeps its own copy of the catalog's value parsing (tests keep them in step on a fixed corpus). Limits bound one evaluation, not request rate. The compiler trusts its catalog to hold published attributes only, and attribute authorization is not implemented | M | M | Keep it labelled as a proof of concept; before any use add storage, hash verification on load, parity tests and maker-checker (IAM-WF-019); complete evidence with protected snapshot references and restrict who can read it; business sign-off on DL-008 and a benchmark; rate limits on the later simulation API; decide how publishing treats flagged overlaps | ADR-0004, DL-008, DL-011, DL-040 | IAM Domain lead, Security Architect | Open |
